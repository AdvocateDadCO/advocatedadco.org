Featured resource · Blind and low vision · Complex needs

Communication & Access: Start with the person.

Explore communication and participation supports for blind and low vision people with complex needs. This AdvocateDadCO guide brings together iPad accessibility, augmentative and alternative communication (AAC), tactile and object communication, auditory scanning, text and braille, routines, literacy, and self-advocacy.

For families, caregivers, teachers of students with visual impairments, speech-language pathologists, assistive technology teams, and other support professionals. Use it to compare options and plan an individualized trial.
