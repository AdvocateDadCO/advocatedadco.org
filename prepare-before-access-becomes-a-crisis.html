Prepare before access becomes a crisis

The TSBVI Administrator’s Toolbox gives school leaders a place to prepare before a student arrives.

By Rob Harris · Originally shared on LinkedIn · August 2026

All blog articles

Adapted from Rob’s original LinkedIn post. Added to the website archive on October 29, 2026.

Another fantastic resource from the Texas School for the Blind and Visually Impaired (TSBVI).

If you are an administrator, educator, related service provider, or special education leader, this is a resource worth bookmarking.

Your students need you to understand this work. Your staff need you to understand it. And your system needs leaders who know how to put it into practice.

You may not have a student who is blind or has low vision in your school or district today—but you might tomorrow.

That is not the time to start figuring out what accessible instruction, appropriate services, qualified personnel, and meaningful inclusion should look like.

The TSBVI Administrator’s Toolbox gives education leaders a place to start—and a place to come back to when a student arrives and the team is looking for solutions.

Good systems prepare before the need becomes a crisis.
