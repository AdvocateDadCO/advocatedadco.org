Biographies

About Rob Harris

Short bio

Rob Harris is a Western Colorado father, caregiver, Certified Individualized Education Program (IEP) Facilitator, and systems advocate working across blindness, special education, Medicaid, and youth transition.

Standard bio

Rob Harris is a father, caregiver, Colorado Department of Education (CDE) Certified Individualized Education Program (IEP) Facilitator, and disability-systems advocate from Western Colorado. His work spans blindness and Braille literacy, special education, assistive technology, Medicaid and home- and community-based services (HCBS), and youth self-determination. He brings family experience and a systems background to state and national policy conversations.

Rob and Isabelle

Rob and Isabelle Harris bring complementary perspectives from Western Colorado. Rob connects federal policy and infrastructure to family, rural, and implementation realities. Isabelle is a blind young-adult self-advocate, Braille reader, assistive-technology user, and white-cane traveler who speaks from her own lived experience.
