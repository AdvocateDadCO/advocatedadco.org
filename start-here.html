Start here: practical tools for access and accountability

Whether you are preparing for an IEP meeting at midnight, building accessible instruction in a rural school district, or reviewing disability policy, start with a practical next step.

AdvocateDadCO brings together lived experience and systems advocacy from Rob Harris, a parent and CDE Certified IEP Facilitator.

Choose your starting point

For parents, caregivers, and blind or low-vision young people

Prepare for a meeting, understand evaluations, and find questions that help the team focus on the student’s needs.

  • Blind and low-vision IEP preparation checklist

    Set priorities for access, instruction, and follow-through.

  • Essential IEP evaluations guide

    Understand FVE, LMA, O&M, and assistive technology evaluations.

  • Optic nerve hypoplasia resource hub

    Find medical, hormone, family, and school resources.

  • 100 Colorado parent questions

    Explore education, Medicaid, caregiving, and local resources.

For teachers, TVIs, paraeducators, and providers

Build access and independence into instruction, communication, and transition planning from day one.

  • Expanded Core Curriculum and transition readiness guide

    Connect assessed needs with meaningful goals and preparation for adult life.

  • Communication and access for complex needs

    Explore tactile communication, AAC, auditory scanning, and individualized trials.

  • Everyday assistance guide

    Offer useful assistance while respecting choice and independence.

  • Blind and low-vision access for professionals

    Plan access in services, materials, and everyday interactions.

  • Accessible STEM and braille math resources

    Explore tactile graphics, lab access, and Project INSPIRE.
