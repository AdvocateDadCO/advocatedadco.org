Bring a practical workshop on assistive technology and student independence to your organization or conference.

60-minute paid workshop

From Access to Agency

A student gets a device. Can they use it to read a work schedule, find out about a community event, or ask for an accommodation when the teacher isn’t there?

This workshop is for blindness organizations, parent centers, and educator conferences. It gives families, educators, and transition teams a way to tell whether assistive technology is helping a blind or low vision student do more independently, with the support the student chooses.

Read the article behind the workshop

What attendees take back to their teams

We’ll work through a sample scenario together. Attendees leave with four questions they can use in their next IEP or transition planning meeting.

  1. What does the student want or need to do?
  2. Who will teach the skills and help troubleshoot?
  3. Where will the student practice and show what they can do?
  4. What keeps access working when the tool fails or the student leaves school?

For example, a student could practice reading a work schedule with a screen reader, choose what help they need, and show how they’d catch a missing shift or ask for clarification.
